FAQs

Frequently asked questions

See all FAQs

Your funds sit in a wallet only you control. Splash can't touch, freeze or move them. Trading itself carries risk, so only trade what you can afford to lose.

A multiplier scales how much a price move affects your position, in either direction. A 5× long moves five times as much as the price itself, so gains and losses both grow.

Your position loses value as the price moves the other way. You can close it whenever you like. With a multiplier, a large enough move against you can close the position automatically and result in total loss. Start small while you learn how a market moves.

Following adds a wallet to your feed and lets you track its trades live or configure alerts when it makes a move. Nothing is ever traded for you, and every trade you make is your own call.

Sign in to Splash on your new phone with the email or phone number you signed up with and your wallet is restored. You can also export your private key at any time, and we recommend keeping a copy stored safely offline.
